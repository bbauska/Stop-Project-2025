they can take over the government, impose their agenda, and control every American. They plan

Four Pillars
Project 2025 is a concerted effort by former Trump officials, rightwing operatives, and dark money
backers to lay the groundwork for a second Trump Administration. It has four pillars:
1. Project 2025 Blueprint: The 920-page “Mandate for Leadership” provides detailed policy
and personnel plans for the next president to abolish or restructure every federal agency.
2. Personnel Database: Collects thousands of resumes from MAGA loyalists to replace non-
partisan civil servants with political appointees that pass the MAGA loyalty test.
3. Administration Academy: private training center to shape and prepare MAGA loyalists to
take over government positions and politicize the civil service.
4. First 180 Days Playbook: Secret action plan, including immediate executive orders and
other actions too controversial to publish, to carry out Trump’s promise of being “a dictator
on Day 1.”
Armed with Project 2025, infused with absolute immunity from his Supreme Court, and
unrestrained by the checks and balances that protected certain rights and freedoms during his
first presidency, Trump would have everything he needs to take total control of your life.
